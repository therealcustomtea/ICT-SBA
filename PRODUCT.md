# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Casual puzzle players, students practising logical reasoning, friends who challenge one another, competitive daily players, and teachers demonstrating Mastermind. People should be able to begin as an authenticated guest on mobile or desktop without first creating a permanent account.

## Product Purpose

Cipherboard is a consumer logic and pattern-recognition product for solo, daily, asynchronous friend, pass-and-play, and real-time private Mastermind games. It makes the rules understandable within a minute while preserving strategic depth, privacy, accessibility, and server-authoritative competitive fairness.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and quietly playful. The product feels like a modern logic instrument: focused enough for serious play, warm enough for students and friends, and confident without spectacle.

## Anti-references

It must not resemble a school worksheet, terminal demonstration, generic SaaS dashboard, casino or neon game, childish clip-art product, stock-template landing page, or interface that relies on colour alone. Decorative animation, fake metrics, glassmorphism, and manipulative streak pressure are out of character.

## Design Principles

1. Let a new player begin meaningful play in under a minute.
2. Make familiar controls disappear into the task instead of inventing novel affordances.
3. Keep strategy, feedback, and the next valid action legible without visual overload.
4. Make server authority, competitive fairness, privacy, and connection state visible when they matter.
5. Pair every colour with a stable symbol or pattern, and create quiet moments of delight without pressure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard play, visible focus, screen-reader feedback announcements, colour-blind-safe symbols and patterns, touch targets of at least 44 by 44 pixels, reduced motion, zoom, English and Traditional Chinese, and understandable failure states. The service is intended for users aged 13 and above.
